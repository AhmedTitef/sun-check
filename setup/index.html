Set it up on your phone

About 15 minutes, once. It all runs on your own phone and your own free Google account. Nobody else sees your list, and nothing goes through anyone else's computer.

  1. Make your request form

    Free with any Google account. Easiest on a computer, at forms.google.com.

    • Start a blank form called Solar requests.
    • Add three questions: Name (short answer), Phone (short answer) and Details (paragraph).
    • In Settings, turn off collecting email addresses and anything that needs sign-in.
    • On the Responses tab, link it to a Google Sheet. That Sheet is your record of each request and each person's OK to text.

  2. Connect it to your coach

    • In the form, open the three-dot menu and choose Get pre-filled link.
    • Type NAME, PHONE and DETAILS as the three answers, then Get link and Copy link.
    • In the coach, go to More and paste it into Your Google Form's pre-filled link. It says Connected when it's right.

    Your name, number and form go inside your own code and links, after the # sign. Browsers never send that part to any website, so they're never stored anywhere but your phone.

  3. Get pinged with the details

    Google's own alert only says "new response". This short script, saved in your own Sheet, emails you the name, number and times with a one-tap text link, so you can answer within five minutes.

    • In your Sheet: Extensions, then Apps Script. Paste this in and save.
    function onFormSubmit(e) {
  var v = e.namedValues;
  var name = (v['Name'] || [''])[0];
  var phone = (v['Phone'] || [''])[0];
  var details = (v['Details'] || [''])[0];
  var digits = String(phone).replace(/\D/g, '');
  var html = '<p><b>' + name + '</b> ' + phone + '</p>'
    + '<pre>' + details + '</pre>'
    + '<p><a href="sms:+1' + digits + '">Text ' + name + ' now</a></p>';
  MailApp.sendEmail({
    to: Session.getEffectiveUser().getEmail(),
    subject: 'New solar request: ' + name,
    htmlBody: html
  });
}
    • Open Triggers (the clock icon), add a trigger for onFormSubmit, event source From spreadsheet, type On form submit, and allow it to send email as you.
    • On your phone, turn on notifications for the Gmail app.
    • Test it: open your page on another phone, send a request, and watch for the email.

  4. Put the coach on your home screen

    • Open the coach in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    • It works with no signal in the store, and your maybe list stays on this phone. Tap Save a backup in More now and then.

  5. Share your code

    • On the floor, tap Show my code. Shoppers scan your screen from about a meter away, and it keeps your screen awake while it's up.
    • Printed cards, the counter sign and door hangers are optional, for later, in More.

  6. Clear it with your manager first

    Until you have a yes in writing, use the demo only, with no real customers.

    • Can I use my own page, code and cards, and can they name the company? The OK-to-text wording has to name the company, so real sign-ups wait on this.
    • Can I keep requests and a maybe list in my own Google Sheet and phone, and text from my own number? Company policy treats customer details as confidential.
    • Does time spent texting after hours count as work time? Postings say work outside the store needs approval.
    • Is a rough estimate on my page OK for an appointment setter? In California, quoting prices or savings can turn a setter into a salesperson who has to register.
    • Which privacy note should the page show? California requires one on any page that collects names and numbers.

    Already settled: appointments you book outside the store count for you. Enter every one in the tablet yourself so it's credited to you.
