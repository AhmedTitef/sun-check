# Sun Check (demo)

A demo of a self-serve roof check and a pocket coach for an in-store solar appointment setter.

- `/` is the page shoppers open from the rep's code: their roof from above, a rough estimate, and the times that suit them.
- `/rep/` is the rep's pocket coach: openers, quick answers, score taps, a maybe list and the evening's texts.
- `/print/` holds optional printouts: cards, a counter sign and a door hanger.
- `/setup/` covers how the rep sets it up on her own phone and Google account.

Demo mode: no names, numbers or accounts are stored in this repository. A rep's details live only on her own phone, and inside the `#fragment` of the links she shares, which browsers never send to any server. Nothing here collects real people's information until the rep's manager approves.
